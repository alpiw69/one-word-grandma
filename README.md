## What this is

Exactly one word, the single word grandma would text about AI:

> Al

She read "AI" as a person's name. So now she asks about Al, the nice young man who answers all her questions.

Why Al and not something else: "robot" is what everyone expects grandma to say, and a typo like "robit" is just a misspelling. Al is the actual misunderstanding — she thinks the AI is a person, and she is already on first-name terms with him. It is funny because it is true to how grandmas really talk about new technology.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
